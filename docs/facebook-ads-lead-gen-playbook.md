# Facebook Ads Lead-Gen Playbook (Local Service Businesses)

Source: TikTok walkthrough from a media buyer describing the exact setup they run for
themselves and for client accounts. Captured here as a repeatable SOP.

**Core thesis:** one campaign, one ad set, four ads, tight local targeting, manual
placements, a high-intent instant form, and no AI "enhancements." Rule of thumb from the
video: *if Facebook is recommending it, it's probably a bad idea.*

---

## 1. Campaign level

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | **Leads** |
| Setup type | **Manual campaign setup** (not the guided/automated flow) |
| Advantage+ campaign | **Off** |
| Structure | 1 campaign → 1 ad set → 4 ads |

Do not let Meta auto-build the campaign. Everything below depends on having manual control.

## 2. Ad set level

- **Conversion location: Instant Form.** No landing page, no website. The creative and the
  form do the work.
- **Daily budget: $20–$50/day** to start, scaled to the size of the business.
- **Audience — geography only:**
  - Enter only the cities, ZIP codes, or radius the business actually serves.
  - Bonus: layer in the highest-income ZIP codes in the service area when the offer suits it.
  - Make sure you're targeting *people who live in* the location, not people merely traveling
    through it (Meta's "People living in this location" option).
- **Keep the rest of the audience broad.** No interest stacking, no detailed targeting,
  no lookalikes at this stage. The geo + creative is the targeting.
- **Placements: Manual placements.**
  - **Turn off:** Audience Network, Messenger, Threads.
  - **Keep on (Facebook + Instagram only):** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Facebook Stories,
    Instagram Stories, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels.

## 3. Ads — four inside the one ad set

Build **2 image ads + 2 video ads**. Same offer, same basic copy across all four — you are
testing *creative*, not testing four different businesses or four different offers.

**Video ads (2):** the business owner on camera. Script beats:
1. Who you are / that you own the business.
2. Why people should choose you.
3. Your **unique mechanism** — the specific way you do what you do.

**Image ads (2):** unpolished and real. You in your truck, you smiling in front of your crew.
Not stock, not a designed graphic.

**Every ad's CTA:** fill out the form, tied to a **specific offer with a deadline** (something
that visibly runs out). Time-bound beats generic.

**Turn off all creative enhancements** (Advantage+ creative / creative enhancements), including:
- AI-generated text
- Image expansion
- Visual touch-ups
- Automatic music
- Text improvements
- Anything else that alters the original ad

## 4. The instant lead form

- **Form type: Higher intent** — not "More volume." This adds a review/confirm step at the end
  ("Are you sure you want to submit this information to this business?"), which strips out the
  people who tapped by accident and won't know why you're calling.
- **Fields:** name, phone number, email, what service they need, when they want the service.
- **Add 1–2 qualifying questions** to filter out bad-fit leads. Keep it short.
- **SMS phone verification: not at the start.** Only turn it on if you start seeing broken or
  disconnected numbers — and only if your account offers the option. Add friction reactively;
  starting with heavy friction drives cost per lead up. The goal is low CPL *and* a healthy
  conversion rate.
- **Privacy policy link is required.** Missing it is a common cause of ad-level rejection.
- **Final/thank-you screen: say exactly what happens next.** e.g. "Submit your information and
  we'll call you shortly to discuss your free estimate."

Then publish the campaign.

## 5. Speed to lead

Call every lead as fast as possible — **under 5 minutes is the benchmark.** A large share of the
outcome is just being the first person to call, sounding pleasant, competent, and interested.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Objective: Leads
- [ ] Manual campaign setup, Advantage+ off
- [ ] 1 campaign / 1 ad set / 4 ads
- [ ] Conversion location: Instant Form
- [ ] Daily budget $20–$50
- [ ] Geo-only targeting (service-area cities / ZIPs / radius), people *living* there
- [ ] No detailed interest targeting
- [ ] Manual placements; Audience Network, Messenger, Threads off
- [ ] Feeds + Stories + Reels on FB & IG only
- [ ] 2 image ads + 2 video ads, same offer and copy
- [ ] Deadline-bound offer in every CTA
- [ ] All creative enhancements off on every ad
- [ ] Form set to Higher intent
- [ ] Name, phone, email, service needed, timing + 1–2 qualifiers
- [ ] SMS verification off at launch (add later only if numbers are bad)
- [ ] Privacy policy link attached
- [ ] Thank-you screen states the next step
- [ ] Call every lead in under 5 minutes
