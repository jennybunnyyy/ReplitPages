After your IPL

OptiLight IPL by Lumenis · eye & skin care

What's normal

  • Mild warmth, redness or puffiness for a few hours
  • Temporary blurry vision for up to an hour
  • Pigmented spots darken and look like coffee grounds, then flake off in 7–21 days. Don't pick them.
  • Dry eye symptoms may fluctuate between sessions

First 48 hours

  • Avoid sun. Apply mineral SPF 30+ every 2 hours outdoors.
  • No saunas, hot tubs, hard workouts or hot showers on the face for 24 hrs
  • Skip makeup for 4–6 hrs and retinols, acids or scrubs for 1 week
  • Use a gentle cleanser and moisturizer only
  • Keep up your at-home plan: warm, cleanse, hydrate

Call us if

  • You have blistering, oozing or worsening pain
  • Redness lasts more than 48 hours
  • Your vision changes

After RF & TriLift

TempSure Envi radiofrequency · TriLift muscle stimulation

What's normal

  • Warm, pink skin for 1–2 hours
  • A tight, lifted feeling. That's the collagen response.
  • Mild muscle soreness after TriLift, like after a workout

First 24 hours

  • Drink plenty of water
  • Avoid saunas, hot yoga and intense heat
  • Gentle cleanser and moisturizer, with SPF in the morning
  • Makeup is fine the same day

Best results

  • Complete the full series, about 1 week apart for TriLift and 2–4 weeks apart for RF
  • Collagen keeps building for up to 3 months
  • Maintenance every 3–6 months

Keep your eyes comfortable

  • Warm mask 10–15 min, then lid cleanser
  • Preservative-free drops as directed
  • Omega-3 daily with a meal
