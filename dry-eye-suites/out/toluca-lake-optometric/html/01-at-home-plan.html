Everyday habits that help

20-20-20 rule
Every 20 minutes of screen time, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
Full blinks
Close gently and squeeze for 2 seconds, 10 times, a few times a day.
Protect the air
Use a humidifier, point vents and fans away from your face, and drink plenty of water.

Remove contact lenses before drops unless your doctor says otherwise. If you have pain, sudden vision change or a red, sticky eye, call us right away.
