Understanding dry eye

What is dry eye?

Dry eye is a chronic, progressive condition. Your tears don't keep the front of your eye comfortable and protected, either because you don't make enough or because they evaporate too fast.

In most people the cause is meibomian gland dysfunction (MGD). The tiny oil glands in your eyelids clog, so tears evaporate in seconds.

Why treat it early?

Untreated, inflammation can permanently shrink the oil glands and damage the surface of the eye. Consistent care protects your glands and your vision.
