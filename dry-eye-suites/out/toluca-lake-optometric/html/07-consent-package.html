Intense Pulsed Light (IPL)

Patient
Date of birth
Date

I authorize Toluca Lake Optometric Center to perform OptiLight IPL by Lumenis intense pulsed light treatment on the skin below the eyes and around the nose and cheeks (and the upper lids if indicated) to treat dry eye disease and meibomian gland dysfunction. IPL delivers filtered pulses of light while protective eye shields are worn.

Expected benefits

Improvement in dry eye signs and symptoms. Results vary, a series of treatments is usually needed, and maintenance treatments are recommended. No specific result is guaranteed.

Possible risks & side effects

  • Temporary redness, swelling, warmth or a sunburn-like feeling
  • Darkening and flaking of pigmented spots (expected, 7–21 days)
  • Blistering, crusting or, rarely, scarring
  • Lightening or darkening of skin color (hypo/hyperpigmentation), usually temporary but possibly permanent
  • Loss of eyebrow or facial hair in treated areas
  • Eye injury if shields are removed during treatment
  • Cold sore flare in those with a history

Alternatives

Warm compresses, lid hygiene, artificial tears, prescription eye drops, oral medication, and other in-office gland treatments.

I understand and agree that

  • I have given a complete and honest medical history and will report any changes before each session.
  • I have read the pre-treatment and aftercare instructions and will follow them.
  • I have had the chance to ask questions, and they were answered to my satisfaction.
  • I consent to before/after photos for my medical record. I also allow anonymous use for education/marketing.
  • I understand this treatment is elective and may not be covered by insurance.

Radiofrequency (TempSure Envi)

Patient
Date of birth
Date

I authorize Toluca Lake Optometric Center to perform TempSure Envi radiofrequency treatment of the eyelids and surrounding skin. RF energy gently heats the tissue to melt thickened gland oils, improve meibomian gland function, and stimulate collagen. Eye shields may be used, and the temperature is monitored throughout.

Expected benefits

Improvement in dry eye signs and symptoms. Results vary, a series of treatments is usually needed, and maintenance treatments are recommended. No specific result is guaranteed.

Possible risks & side effects

  • Warmth, redness or mild swelling for a few hours
  • Temporary tingling or tenderness
  • Rarely, a burn, blister or change in skin color
  • Temporary blurred vision or eye irritation
  • Not suitable with pacemakers, implanted electronic devices or metal in the area

Alternatives

Warm compresses, lid hygiene, artificial tears, prescription drops, IPL and other in-office gland treatments.

TriLift Muscle Stimulation

Patient
Date of birth
Date

I authorize Toluca Lake Optometric Center to perform TriLift treatment. Applicators deliver gentle electrical muscle stimulation to tone the muscles around the eyes, support a more complete blink, and improve the look of the lids and surrounding skin.

Expected benefits

Improvement in dry eye signs and symptoms. Results vary, a series of treatments is usually needed, and maintenance treatments are recommended. No specific result is guaranteed.

Possible risks & side effects

  • Tingling, warmth or muscle twitching during treatment
  • Muscle soreness for 1–2 days
  • Temporary redness under the applicator
  • Rarely, skin irritation or a burn
  • Not suitable with pacemakers, implanted electronic devices, metal implants in the face, pregnancy or epilepsy

Alternatives

Blink exercises, lid hygiene, artificial tears, prescription drops and other in-office dry eye treatments.
