## Must confirm before anything goes out
- [ ] **$300M figure:** GMV (sales through the platform) or company revenue? Word it to match.
- [ ] **"1,000+ practices":** active partners today, or cumulative sign-ups?
- [ ] **LOI confidentiality:** can you mention a pending sale at all? (Assume not.)
- [ ] **Deal restrictive covenants:** ask your attorney whether a role at Opus (or any eye-care company) falls under the non-compete, non-solicit or transition terms.
- [ ] **Exit wording:** only after close, and match the acquirer's public announcement.
- [ ] **Overlap check:** are Techify sponsors, Dr. Contact Lens/Visibly, or your practices customers, vendors or competitors of Opus?
- [ ] **Clinical schedule:** how many days/week you see patients now, and the coverage plan if you take a W-2 role.
